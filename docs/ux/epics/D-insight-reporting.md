# Epic D — Insight & reporting

Persona: [Péter](../personas/peter-selfhoster.md). Progressive disclosure: simple views
first, pivot/statistics as advanced tools.

### UX-D1 (P1, draft)

**As** a user, **I want** a month-over-month comparison without learning what a pivot
table is, **so that** insight does not require training.

Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A default simple view shows month-over-month totals and top categories; pivot and
      statistics are the advanced layer behind it.

### UX-D2 (P2, draft)

**As** a user, **I want** to export a report (CSV or print), **so that** I can share the
results with a partner or accountant.

Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] At least one shareable export format is available from the results view.
